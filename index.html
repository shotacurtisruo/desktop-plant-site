Desktop Plant

A tiny living plant for your Mac desktop. It sways, sleeps when you step away, and slowly grows while you work.

Home Privacy Support

Desktop Plant puts a small, beautiful plant on your desktop. It's alive, and it notices you.

Leaves sway in a gentle breeze and part as your cursor passes through them. Start typing and it perks up; settle into deep work and it calms down so it never pulls your attention. Step away and it slowly falls asleep, then stretches awake when you come back. At night its leaves fold up and a few fireflies blink nearby.

It begins as a seed and grows only while you're using your Mac, sprouting in the first few minutes and filling out over a few days. Water it with a little watering can, tap its leaves, or double-click for a wave. Sometimes something rare happens.

  • Three plants: Pothos, Pilea and Olive
  • Small, medium or large; floating above windows or resting on the desktop
  • Gentle optional sounds, a low-power mode, and open at login
  • No accounts, no ads, no notifications, no timers to wait out
  • Private by design: no data is collected and nothing leaves your Mac
