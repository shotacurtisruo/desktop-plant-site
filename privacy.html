Privacy Policy

Happa does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Home Privacy Support
  • No data collection. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No network access. The app makes no network requests.
  • What it reads, locally: the cursor position (to animate the leaves near it), the number of seconds since your last key press or mouse movement (to know when you're active or away), and the local time of day (for daylight and night). It never reads which keys you press, what you type, or which apps you use.
  • What it stores, locally: your plant's growth, soil moisture, a few counters, its window position, and your settings. These stay on your Mac in the app's own storage and are removed if you delete the app.
