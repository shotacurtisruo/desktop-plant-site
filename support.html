Common questions

  • Where is the app? Happa lives in the menu bar — look for the leaf icon. The plant itself sits on your desktop (bottom-right by default); drag its pot to move it anywhere.
  • How fast does it grow? Only while you're actually using your Mac: it sprouts within the first 15 minutes and is fully grown after about 20 hours of use, roughly 3–5 days. It never dies.
  • How do I water it? Hover over the plant and a watering can appears beside the pot. Drag it over the leaves to pour. Click the soil to check how moist it is.
  • Can it start over? Yes — click the leaf in the menu bar and choose Reset plant. It asks first.
  • Why no permission prompts? The app needs none. It reads only the system idle time and the cursor position, collects nothing, and never touches the network.
